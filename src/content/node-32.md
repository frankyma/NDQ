Bhakti Yoga is the path of devotion and love of the divine.

It asks us that we have no love but the love of God (Brahman). And in turn, this love permeates through to everyone and everything. It becomes easier to shed our worldly attachments.

"The experience of suffering is the fullest expression of God's love." - Father Stu

One can choose to show their devotion to a god with form (Saguna Brahman), such as The Divine Mother, Jesus, Allah, etc. Or to eternal consciousness (Nirguna Brahman).
