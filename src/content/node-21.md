_All the Buddhas and all sentient beings are nothing but the One Mind, beside which nothing exists._

― The Zen Teaching of Huang Po: On the Transmission of Mind

Where Madhyamaka examines the nature of objects, Yogacara shifts the question: what is actually present in experience?

Its answer: only consciousness (_vijnana_). The external world as we perceive it is not independent reality — it is a construction of mind.

## The Mind-Only Teaching (Cittamatra)

Yogacara's core claim is that what we take to be an "outer world" is never directly encountered. Every experience — sights, sounds, sensations, thoughts — arises within consciousness. The apparent subject-object split, the sense that there is a perceiver here and a world out there, is itself a projection of mind.
