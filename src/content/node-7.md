Christian mysticism is the contemplative tradition within Christianity that seeks direct, experiential union with the divine — beyond doctrine and intellectual belief.

## Apophatic Theology (Via Negativa)

The *via negativa* holds that the divine is ultimately incomprehensible. No positive description can fully capture God — not even "good," "powerful," or "existing" in the ordinary sense. We can only say what God is *not*. This is not skepticism but a recognition of the infinite excess of the divine over all concepts.

## Neoplatonism

The philosopher Plotinus (204–270 CE) described all existence as an emanation from **The One** — an undivided, indescribable source beyond being itself. All things proceed from The One, and the soul's deepest movement is a return to that original unity.

This framework profoundly shaped Christian mystical thought, offering a philosophical vocabulary for the soul's journey toward union.
