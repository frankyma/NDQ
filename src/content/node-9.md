Biology studies living systems: how organisms sense, respond, adapt, and persist. At first glance it seems to reinforce a familiar story — that consciousness arises only in brains, and only in animals like us.

Look closer, and that story frays. Plants decide without neurons. A surgically divided brain can host two streams of knowing under one skull. The neat boundary between conscious mind and mindless matter begins to look like a useful fiction rather than a fact of nature.

Biology cannot prove non-duality. But it can show where our assumptions about who — and what — can be aware no longer hold.
