_"The universe is just mindstuff."_ -Sir Arthur Eddington

We typically believe that for something to exist, it must have size. However fundamental particles, such as electrons and quarks, do not inherently have volume. According to the standard model, these building blocks are point-like particles. This means they are strictly mathematical points in space with no physical dimensions or internal structure.

We also instinctively believe that existence, meaning having "weight" or "heft", or in the words of physics, mass. However, for the building blocks of matter, mass itself is not intrinsic. Particles such as electrons acquire mass through interaction with the **Higgs field**.

The solid, self-existing world dissolves under examination.
