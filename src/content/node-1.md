Non-duality is a modern word for an ancient truth: reality is not fundamentally split, not into self and other, subject and object, or a God apart from the world.

Almost every difficulty we carry rests on that sense of separation — the fear of losing what we have, the ache of wanting what we don't, the loneliness of a small self set against a vast world. When separation is seen through, the ground of that suffering falls away.

This is something to be discovered, but not from elsewhere. No belief to adopt and nothing to acquire. It is already what we are, waiting only to be recognized. Philosophy, religion, and science each arrive here by different routes.
