_"The only thing that separates you from God is the thought that you are separate from God."_ — Richard Rohr

Richard Rohr is one of the most prominent modern Christian mystics. He is a Franciscan friar and the founder of the Center for Action and Contemplation in Albuquerque, New Mexico.

His central teaching is the **Universal Christ**. For Rohr, “Christ” is not simply Jesus’ last name. It names the divine presence woven into all of creation: God taking form in matter, humanity, and everything that exists.

Jesus is the clearest revelation of this reality, fully human and fully divine at once. Classical theology calls this the **hypostatic union**. Rohr sees it not only as something unique to Jesus, but as a revelation of the pattern of reality itself: matter and spirit were never truly separate.

What keeps us from recognizing this is the **False Self**, the identity we build from our roles, wounds, reputation, and achievements. Spiritual practice asks this small self to die, not by destroying the person, but by letting go of the belief that the ego is the whole of who we are. The **True Self** is not a better ego. It is our deeper identity in God, revealed when the ego stops insisting that it is the whole story.

We do not create union with God; we wake up to the union that is already there.
