_Advaita Vedanta is "a poetic echo" of what physics points toward_ - Brian Greene (_Until the End of Time_)

The illusion of time and space creates the apparence of separation. We believe there are things "out there" and that I am "over here." We also believe that there is a "then" that's apart from the "now".

However, Einstein has shown us that space and distance are relative. The faster we move, the more time slows down and distances shrink.

A photon, which travels at the speed of light, does not experience time or distance. From its perspective, a photo leaves the sun and and arrives on earth in an instant, and the eight minutes, 150 million kilometers journey collapses to a single event. For a photon, the entirety universe and all of its events exist in the now.

We think we are made of matter, but Einstein has shown that matter and light (energy) is fundamentally interchangeable. E=mc² equates energy and mass. Scientists have used particle accelerators to turn light energy into matter (and antimatter), and vice-versa. So inherently, we are made of the same substance as this energy which experiences everything in the here and now.
