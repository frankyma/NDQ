_Advaita Vedanta is "a poetic echo" of what physics points toward_ - Brian Greene (_Until the End of Time_)

The illusion of time and space creates the illusion of separation. We believe there are things "out there" and that I am "over here." Because there is a distance between us, it takes time to get from here to there.

However, Einstein has shown us that space and distance are relative. The faster we move, the more time slows down and distances shrink.

A photon, which travels at the speed of light, does not experience time or distance. From its frame of reference, the universe has zero size and zero duration. From here on Earth, the light takes seven minutes to get here from the sun. But from the photon's perspective, it is created and arrives here in an instant, and the 150 million kilometers it has traveled collapses. For a photon, the entire universe and all of its events exist in the now.
