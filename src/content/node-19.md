Consciousness (Vijnana) is the momentary awareness of an object through a sense door or the mind.

Our eye consciousness allows us to see the vivid red of an apple. As we bring it to our lips, our taste consciousness experiences the burst of sweet and citrus. There is no self in this moment-to-moment experience.

Note: In Vedanta terms, this refers to reflected consciousness (chidabhasa), not ultimate consciousness (Sat-Cit-Ananda).
